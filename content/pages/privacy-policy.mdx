This policy explains how BreathWave handles information when you use our iPhone and Apple Watch apps, including breathing practice, membership and Deep Dive audio.

## Account and membership information

When you sign in, we process your account identifier, email address and any name supplied by your sign-in provider to authenticate you and manage your account. Supabase provides account and profile services. If you purchase or restore a membership, Apple processes the payment. We and our purchase service provider, RevenueCat, process transaction identifiers and membership status to provide the features you purchased. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Account identifiers, contact information and purchase records may be linked to your account or device. They are not anonymous data.

## Breathing practice and Apple Health

Breathing history and audio playback preferences are stored on your device. With your permission, the app accesses Apple Health and Apple Watch data, such as heart rate and mindfulness or workout records, for practice guidance and summaries. The app can exchange practice information between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch.

The app does not upload your heart-rate measurements or breathing-session history to our account, audio-delivery or analytics servers. Health permissions are optional and can be managed in Apple Health or system settings. Any Apple Health or device-backup synchronization with iCloud is controlled by your Apple settings.

We do not use Apple Health data for advertising.

## Audio delivery and offline listening

Deep Dive audio can be delivered through Apple-hosted asset packs or our Cloudflare storage and delivery service. To authorize access to full audio, our service may process an authenticated account request or an Apple-signed transaction, the requested track identifier and membership expiry. A temporary access link is used to deliver the requested audio.

Network requests include technical connection information such as an IP address. Our audio Worker does not enable application request logging or persist the authorization payload as a listening-history database. Hosting providers may process technical information to operate and secure their services.

Downloaded audio stays on your device until removed through the app or by the operating system.

## Usage information and notifications

OpenPanel provides app usage analytics, including app interactions and technical device or app information. OneSignal provides push-notification delivery when enabled, using a device or subscription identifier and, after sign-in, an account identifier. RevenueCat also provides purchase-related analytics. These identifiers and interactions may be associated with your device or account.

You can change notification permission in system settings. The app does not use collected information to track you across other companies' apps or websites for targeted advertising.

## Service providers and security

Apple, Supabase, RevenueCat, OneSignal, OpenPanel and Cloudflare process information as needed to deliver their respective services. Information may be processed outside your country or region. We use HTTPS for network communication and access controls for account and membership services. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information where needed to comply with legal obligations, protect users or the service, or with your consent.

## Retention, deletion and your choices

We retain account information while needed to provide your account and services. You can request account deletion through the account controls in the app or contact us. Some transaction or security records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

You can manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Deleting an app account does not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. You can remove downloaded audio in the app and manage Apple Health records in Apple Health. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to your device-backup settings.

## Changes and contact

We will update this page when our privacy practices change. The date above identifies this version.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact [support@breathwave.app](mailto:support@breathwave.app).
