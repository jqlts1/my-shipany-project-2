## Introduction

Welcome to BreathWave, a professional breathing training app designed to help you master scientific breathing techniques. Your privacy is important to us, and this policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

## Information Collection and Use

We collect the following types of data to provide you with a better experience:

1. **Account Information**
   - **What We Collect**: Your name, email address, and account credentials.
   - **Purpose**: To manage your account and provide customer support.

2. **Health Data**
   - **What We Collect**: Heart rate, HRV (Heart Rate Variability), and breathing session data from Apple Watch.
   - **Purpose**: To track your breathing practice progress and provide personalized insights.

3. **Usage Details**
   - **What We Collect**: Information about how you use BreathWave, including session frequency and duration.
   - **Purpose**: To analyze user engagement and improve our services.

4. **Device Information**
   - **What We Collect**: Device type, operating system, and app version.
   - **Purpose**: To optimize our services and ensure compatibility.

## Health Data Privacy

We take your health data privacy seriously:

- Health data is stored locally on your device and in your iCloud account
- We do not sell or share your health data with third parties
- You can delete your health data at any time through the app settings
- Health data syncs with Apple Health only with your explicit permission

## Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely with encryption and access controls. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect against unauthorized access.

## Information Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information except:

- To comply with legal obligations
- To protect our rights and safety
- With your explicit consent

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
