In this "always-on" era, anxiety seems to be standard for modern life. Dense notifications, endless meetings, late-night blue light... our nervous systems are chronically in a tight stress response state.

Many believe meditation or relaxation requires large blocks of time and a quiet environment, which is a luxury for busy urbanites. But actually, "micro-breathing" exercises done during "fragmented time" can bring immediate results.

## What is "Micro-Breathing" Training?

"Micro-breathing" refers to high-quality conscious breathing practiced in the gaps of daily life for 1-3 minutes. It doesn't require sitting cross-legged or lighting scented candles; it only requires you to temporarily pull your attention back to your breath.

Here is a 24/7 breathing guide designed for the busy modern person:

### 08:00 AM | Wake Up Brain: Wim Hof Breathing

Instead of relying on that first shot of espresso, try a round of Wim Hof breathing.
**Scenario**: Just woke up, or on the commute (not driving).
**Effect**: Increases blood oxygen through hyperventilation, naturally boosts adrenaline, making you instantly awake and ready to start the day with vitality.

### 10:30 AM | Pre-Meeting: Box Breathing

Heart racing before typical pressure or an important speech?
**Scenario**: At your desk, or waiting outside the meeting room.
**Effect**: The 4-4-4-4 rhythm quickly calms the heart rate, helping you regain calm and focus to face challenges with composure.

### 03:00 PM | Afternoon Reboot: Coherent Breathing

Feeling sleepy in the afternoon, attention scattering?
**Scenario**: During lunch break, put on headphones.
**Effect**: Balances the autonomic nervous system, not only relieving eye strain but also giving the brain deep rest in a short time, restoring creativity.

### 11:00 PM | Night Shutdown: 4-7-8 Breathing

Tossing and turning in bed, brain won't stop?
**Scenario**: Lights out, under the covers.
**Effect**: Powerfully activates the parasympathetic nervous system, telling the body "it's sleep time," shortening sleep latency and improving sleep quality.
