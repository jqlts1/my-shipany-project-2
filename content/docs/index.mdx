## What is BreathWave?

BreathWave is a professional breathing training app designed to help you master scientific breathing techniques and improve your physical and mental health.

## Core Features

### Breathing Techniques

- **Wim Hof Method**: A powerful breathing technique created by the "Ice Man" that enhances immunity and stress resistance
- **4-7-8 Breathing**: A relaxation technique that helps relieve anxiety and improve sleep quality

### Apple Watch Support

- Independent Watch app for practice anytime
- Real-time heart rate monitoring during sessions
- HRV (Heart Rate Variability) tracking
- Haptic feedback for breathing rhythm guidance

### Health Data Integration

- Sync with Apple Health
- Track your breathing practice history
- Monitor heart rate and HRV trends
- View detailed session statistics

## Getting Started

1. Download BreathWave from the App Store
2. Choose your preferred breathing technique
3. Follow the visual and haptic guidance
4. Track your progress over time
