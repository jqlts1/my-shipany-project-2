# BreathWave PSEO Architecture Ledger (Source of Truth)

This document defines the strict, high-level Programmatic SEO (PSEO) category hierarchy for the BreathWave application.

**AI AGENT INSTRUCTION:**
When generating new PSEO content (via the `generate-page` workflow), you MUST map the user's Long-Tail Keywords into ONE of the following precise core Hubs.
Do NOT invent new top-level directories or categories without explicit user permission. If a keyword doesn't fit perfectly, default to the closest semantic Hub.

---

## 1. 助眠放松 (Sleep Hub)
- **Directory Path**: `/sleep`
- **Strategic Positioning**: Content focused on lowering heart rate, calming the mind, preparing for sleep, and combating insomnia.
- **Allowed Keywords/Intents**: Deep sleep, 4-7-8 technique, Box breathing for sleep, waking up in middle of night...

## 2. 急救与抗焦虑 (Anxiety Hub)
- **Directory Path**: `/anxiety`
- **Strategic Positioning**: "Fire extinguisher" content. High-urgency scenarios dealing with sudden panic, severe stress, flight-or-fight responses, or performance anxiety.
- **Allowed Keywords/Intents**: Panic attacks, fear of flying, public speaking anxiety, exam stress, sensory overload...

## 3. 专注与高能 (Energy Hub)
- **Directory Path**: `/energy`
- **Strategic Positioning**: Content targeting productivity, removing brain fog, awakening the body, and daytime focus.
- **Allowed Keywords/Intents**: Wim Hof method, focus for coders, morning fatigue, afternoon slump...

## 4. 运动与身体舒缓 (Body Hub)
- **Directory Path**: `/body`
- **Strategic Positioning**: Non-medical relief for specific physical sensations or optimization for athletes. (Caution: Always include medical disclaimers; avoid "cure" or "treat").
- **Allowed Keywords/Intents**: Lower back pain relaxation, breathing for runners, swimmers lung capacity, acid reflux relief...

## 5. 常规资讯与教育 (Blog Hub)
- **Directory Path**: `/blog`
- **Strategic Positioning**: Catch-all for general educational content, science breakdowns, app updates, or broad topics that don't fit a specific actionable scenario.
- **Allowed Keywords/Intents**: History of pranayama, what is HRV, how Apple Watch tracks breathing...
